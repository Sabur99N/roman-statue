06 — The Ages

Two thousand years, four wings

  1. Archaic 700 – 480 BC

    The dawn of monumental stone — rigid grace and the first divine smiles.

  2. Classical 480 – 323 BC

    Perfect proportion and contrapposto; the human form idealised in marble.

  3. Hellenistic 323 – 31 BC

    Drama and emotion — drapery in motion, gods rendered achingly human.

  4. Imperial 31 BC – 476 AD

    Rome's marble empire: portraiture, myth and the cult of the divine.
